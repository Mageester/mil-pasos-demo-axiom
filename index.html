The Heart of Mil Pasos

JULIE + RAFA

A Franco-Cuban dynamic duo uniting authentic Havana roots with Parisian performance discipline.

Mil Pasos was born out of a shared obsession with rhythm, human movement, and genuine community. Julie brought her background in European dance pedagogy, event animation, and fitness. Rafa brought the living heartbeat of Cuban street culture, Capoeira, and Casino. Together in Toronto, they’ve created a studio where anyone—from total beginners to seasoned performers—can discover authentic Latin movement without pretension.

Born in France, Julie began dancing at age 5 in Paris, training in ballet, modern jazz, hip hop, African dance, and salsa. She holds a European diploma in Animation & Event Management and is a certified trainer.

  • Selected dancer for Madonna’s "Addicted to Sweat" cardio dance concept at Hard Candy Toronto.
  • Performed live on stage with Madonna in 2014.
  • Spearheads Mil Pasos’ Zumba, Latin fusion, and Only WomXn styling programs.

Born and raised in Cuba, Rafa’s dance was forged in the streets, social circles, and musical culture of the island.

  • Specialist in authentic Cuban Salsa (Casino) & Timba, with rich foundation in Afro-Cuban movement.
  • Trained in Capoeira and urban movement, bringing unmatched agility, rhythm, and stage presence.
  • Renowned for infectious energy, playful partner lead technique, and making every student feel at home.
